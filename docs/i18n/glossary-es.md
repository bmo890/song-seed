# Spanish glossary

Neutral Spanish with **tú** (never usted, never vosotros) that reads naturally
in Spain and Latin America; avoid regionalisms ("vos", "coger", "ordenador").
Verbs: "agregar" over "añadir". Quotes: « ». Flag 🇪🇸, dates es-ES.
