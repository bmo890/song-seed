# Portuguese (Brazil) glossary

Brazilian Portuguese with **você** (never tu, never o senhor), the way Brazilian
music apps talk to musicians. Current spelling (the 1990 orthographic
agreement). English studio words Brazilian musicians actually use stay English
(take, loop, setlist, playlist). Quotes: “ ”. Flag 🇧🇷, dates pt-BR.
Prefer short natural Brazilian phrasing over European forms ("celular", not
"telemóvel"; "você", not "tu"; gerund "gravando", not "a gravar").
