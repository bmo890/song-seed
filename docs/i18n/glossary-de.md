# German glossary

Standard German with **du** (lowercase in running text, never Sie), the way
German music apps and DAWs speak to musicians. English studio words German
musicians actually use stay English (Take, Clip, Loop, Setlist, Marker).
Quotes: „ “. Flag 🇩🇪, dates de-DE. Compounds are fine but keep buttons to
one or two words.
