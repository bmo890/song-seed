The practice page, tidied

Real phone size (390 × 844 pt, the smaller iPhone). Both play. Tap the dots, the notes, the + and the "…". Four bars of eighths fit with no scrolling, and the tempo stays in view.

What changed

  • Display: with Practice on, the four dots go (the bars show where you are). The number shrinks a little, and "BPM · Moderato" sits on one line beneath it.
  • One control line: Your pattern and Random become two icons (a pattern of dots, a shuffle). Sixteenths, triplets and so on become the Meter sheet's own note glyphs. That's two rows of words turned into one quiet row.
  • Bars: a quiet + sits where the next bar would appear. Removing a bar lives in "…".
  • Sound all / Silence all: both move into the one "…" button. You once found a single key that flips between the two confusing, so each keeps its own clear row in the menu.
  • Random: the same line carries the mode icons, then Few · Some · Many.
