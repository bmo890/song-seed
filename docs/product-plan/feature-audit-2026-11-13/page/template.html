Decide these first

Nineteen places where the split is leaky, uneven or unconfirmed. Answer by number. "Checked twice" means I confirmed it in the code myself after the reader reported it.

Holes in the paywall

  1. Pins are paid in one place and free in threeChecked twice
    Adding a pin while listening is Pro. Dropping one while recording is free, and anyone can rename, move or delete pins. Sections, the richer tool, are free.
    I suggest: make pins free everywhere. The gate does not hold, and marking your own take is tidying, like trimming.
  2. The one free layer can be repeated
    A free person can record a layer, combine it into the take, and record another, again and again.
    I suggest: leave it. Combining is permanent, so it is a slow workaround, not real multitrack.
  3. The three-workspace limit has side doorsChecked twice
    Only the + button checks. Keeping something a friend sent as its own workspace, importing an archive, and restoring a backup can all add more.
    I suggest: leave it. Receiving and restoring must never be blocked.
  4. A word game try is used the moment the game opens
    Open Word Ladder, back out with nothing written, and one of the three tries is gone for good.
    I suggest: count the try only once something is written.
  5. Only the phone counts the 3 Send linksChecked twice
    The server is not checking yet. A free person who pairs a browser can send from a computer without limit. After the switch, the server would also stop founding members at 3 links.
    I suggest: flip the two server switches on launch day, as planned, and decide then whether founding members get unlimited links.

Paid today. Should it be?

  1. Undoing asks for Pro
    "Reset pitch" opens the Pro page. So does stepping a capo or a transpose back down, though taking it off entirely is free.
    I suggest: going back toward the original is always free.
  2. The whole Word Finder is Pro
    All twelve tools, in every language. Only Hebrew meaning lookups cost us money, about a fifth of a cent per new word. German and Portuguese run on the phone.
    I suggest: make Rhymes free and keep the other eleven tools Pro. A writer meets it every session, and the paid tools sit one tab away.
  3. PDF is Pro even for plain lyrics
    It is the only print-ready sheet in the app. Our terms say "export is never locked".
    I suggest: keep PDF Pro and correct the terms: your work always leaves as text, audio and SongNook files.
  4. Capo can show the Pro page on the recording screen
    The lyrics panel in the recorder has the same Aa sheet as everywhere else, capo included.
    I suggest: leave it. It is one rule everywhere, and nothing about recording itself is blocked.
  5. A friend's song loses free transpose once you keep it
    Transpose and capo are free while a song sits in Received. Moving it into your own workspace makes them Pro.
    I suggest: leave it for now. It is rare, and fixing it means marking songs for good.

Free today. Should it be?

  1. Export for DAW
    A bar-aligned WAV with the tempo in its name, ready for Logic. Free, from any clip's Share menu.
    I suggest: move it to Pro. It is a finishing tool, and sharing the plain audio stays free.
  2. The layer mixer
    Level, tone, solo, mute, timing nudge and merge are all free on any layer.
    I suggest: keep it free. It is what makes the one free layer sound right.
  3. Tempo changes inside a song
    Planning tempo or meter changes at chosen bars, which the click follows. The most advanced control in the recorder, and free.
    I suggest: keep it free. We said we never charge for capture.
  4. The daily companions
    Tuner, the full metronome, Bluetooth calibration, sound from videos, automatic backup, playlists, Shelf, Revisit, Activity, and the stage reader with its click. All free, no limits.
    I suggest: keep all of it free. These are why someone opens SongNook every day.
  5. Send from a computer
    Free people can use it inside their 3 links. Our pricing record says it is for paid users.
    I suggest: keep it as built and correct the record.

Sold, but not there

  1. Tempo detectionChecked twice
    Our records list it as a Pro tool. Its button has been hidden since October because the result was not reliable.
    I suggest: take it out of every list of Pro tools until it comes back.
  2. Move an archived workspace to FilesChecked twice
    Pro, but archiving was shelved in August, so nobody new can reach it.
    I suggest: take it off the Pro list until archiving returns.
  3. Step up lets you build a drill, then asks for Pro
    The plan sheet opens for everyone. The Pro page appears at the switch.
    I suggest: keep it. Seeing the drill is a fair taste.
  4. Speed is free to hear and Pro to keep
    Playing at 0.75× is free. Saving a 0.75× copy in the editor is Pro.
    I suggest: keep it. It is a clean line.

Built, but nobody can reach it

The code exists and no button leads to it. None of these are counted above.

  • Tempo detection. Hidden since 13 October until it can be trusted.
  • Archiving a workspace. Shelved on 4 August. Unarchive and "Move package to Files" only appear on a workspace that was already archived.
  • Named Step up plans. Saving a drill under a name is switched off. One custom plan is remembered.
  • "New collection from import" on the workspace page. It works from the share sheet only.
  • Lyrics with or without, as a collection filter. The filter works, but the menu has no control for it.
  • Sorting collections on the workspace page. The words exist; the control does not.
  • Activity: created only or updated only. Fixed to both.
  • Cut-Up by line, phrase or word. Replaced by tapping where to cut.
  • Borrowed Words: "Rebuild from words" and dragging words into order. Never wired to a button.
  • Folding a single layer lane. Only "Collapse all" is on screen.

Wording that is already out of date

Found by the sixth reader, who listed 121 places where we tell people what the app does or costs. I will draft a fix for each and show you before anything is published.

  • Pro page, third line. "Practice tools and the full editor". Cutting is free since today, so "the full editor" now means only Speed & pitch.
  • Terms of use. "Export is never locked", while chart PDF is Pro. It also says "the Pro sheet", which is a full page now.
  • The Share menu hint for Send. "A link anyone can play". The web page offers downloads, not playback.
  • Welcome tour, last page. Says importing is one tap from the + button. Import is in the collection's "…" menu.
  • Website. "Coming to the App Store and Google Play". Wrong from the day we release.
  • The screenshot Apple reviews for the purchases. Shows the old Pro sheet with six lines and "Not now". Only Apple's reviewer sees it.
  • The lifetime launch date. The Pro page prints 9 February in Europe and Israel and 8 February in the Americas.
  • Our own working agreement. Says deleted things wait 14 days. The app keeps them 30.
  • The limits "3 links" and "3 tries". Typed out separately in the terms, the Send page and the server. Changing a limit means changing each one by hand.
